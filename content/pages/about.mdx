Lummi Bay Market is one company with three stores. Each one keeps the name
people already use for it — Exit 260, the Minimart and Fisherman's Cove.

The flagship at Exit 260 also has a **truck stop on the same property**. It is a
separate fuel station for drivers rather than a corner of the store: its own
diesel lanes, its own small c-store, its own hours and its own phone.

## One site instead of three

Each store used to keep a website of its own. They are now a single site, which
means a fuel price, a phone number or a set of opening hours is written once and
is right everywhere it appears — on the store's own page, on
[Contact](/contact), and in the price block at the top of every screen.
