## This website

**lummibay.com sets no cookies and asks for nothing.** There is no account, no login, no
newsletter and no form. Visits are counted anonymously, with no cookie and no cross-site
identifier, and that is the whole of what this site collects. There is no consent banner
because there is nothing to consent to.

Everything below this line is about **the Lummi Bay Market app**, which is a separate
product and does collect personal information. The two are described separately on purpose:
the sentence in the footer of every page on this site is a claim about this site, and it
stays true.
