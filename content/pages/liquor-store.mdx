**Hours:** 8:00am – Midnight

Located inside Lummi Bay Market at Exit 260 in Bellingham, our liquor store offers more than 500 choices and sells until midnight. That makes it easy to grab a bottle on your way home, before a party, or late in the evening. Our shelves cover every category: bourbon, whiskey, Scotch, Irish whiskey, vodka, gin, rum, tequila, brandy, and liqueurs.

We carry everyday favorites like Taaka, Bacardi, Captain Morgan, Fireball, and Everclear, along with premium picks like Crown Royal, Tito's, Grey Goose, Patrón, Hennessy, and The Balvenie. Sizes range from shooters and pints to 750 ml and 1.75 L bottles. We also stock margarita, piña colada, daiquiri, and Bloody Mary mixes, plus grenadine, so you can get everything for your drinks in one stop.
