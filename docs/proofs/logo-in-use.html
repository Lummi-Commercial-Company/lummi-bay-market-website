The lockup, on every surface it lands on

Two PNG files carry the Lummi Bay Market identity across the site. Which one a surface takes is decided by one thing only — the colour of the ground behind it. Everything below is the real artwork at the real size it renders at.

The only rule

The filename names the background, never the ink

on-dark holds the white wordmark. on-light holds the navy one. The name answers “what do I put this on”, so it cannot be read backwards.

logo-market-on-dark.png White wordmark · 8.28:1 on navy · 336 × 120
logo-market-on-light.png Navy wordmark · 8.00:1 on bone · 512 × 512

These were briefly called logo-market and logo-market-reversed, and got swapped — “reversed” reads as either the ink or the ground, and the two readings are opposites. The swap would have shipped a navy wordmark on the navy header: 1.6:1, invisible, on every page.

The home hero

The one place the lockup is the largest thing on the page. The on-dark file cannot appear here — a white wordmark on bone barely registers — which is why PNG-only still means two files rather than one.

The air above and below the lockup here is the file, not the layout. on-light is a 512 × 512 square with the lockup band across its middle and roughly 65% of its height empty. Sized by width it renders 146px tall to show 46px of logo. Harmless in a hero that has room; worth knowing before it goes anywhere tight, where it wants cropping to its ink first.

Density

Same slot, two files

High-density screens fetch the 3× export; everything else fetches the small one. The markup names both and the browser chooses — nothing in the code branches. Both panes below are 112 × 40 on screen.

logo-market-on-dark.png
336 × 120 · 15.6 KB · served at 1×

logo-market-on-dark@3x.png
1008 × 360 · 61.6 KB · served at 3×

Never point src at the 3× file. That ships 61.6 KB to fill a 112px slot on every device. In a srcset it reaches only the screens that can use it.

Resolved — one question left

The tab now holds a mark, not a smear

A new favicon set was supplied on 27 Aug: the paddle device alone, white on a navy disc. It replaces the old set, which carried the full 3:1 lockup squeezed into a square and stopped being a logo at tab size. These are rendered at the true sizes a browser uses, from the real files.

Lummi Bay Market

16px — the common case, hand-tuned

Lummi Bay Market

32px — retina tab

48px — bookmark bar

The legibility problem is solved — the disc reads at all three sizes. The open question is permission, not craft. The recorded ruling was that the paddle on its own is not acceptable as a standalone mark, which is why A11 asked for a monogram or another device. The supplied favicon is the paddle on its own.

If ownership has since approved the paddle as the icon mark, the ruling is out of date and A11 closes — say so and it gets recorded. If not, this set carries the same defect the old one did, in a form that is harder to notice because it looks right. Nothing ships either way until that is confirmed.
