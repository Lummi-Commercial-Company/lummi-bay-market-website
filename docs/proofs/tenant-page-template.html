What staff type

name, headerImage and its alt text, placement, status, a short description, hours with a confirmed date, and any links. That is the whole form.

What they cannot touch

The disclosure line is a property of the template, not a block. A page in our theme carrying another company's name is exactly where forgetting it would be easiest, so it is not available to forget.

The header image is gated

A markApproved flag has to be set before any third-party logo or supplied photograph renders. Until then the page falls back to the business name in our own type, which is always safe to publish.

Adding a company

Create a document in the tenants collection. The page exists at its own URL immediately, with every piece of site furniture already in place. Nothing is rebuilt and no engineer is involved.

Opening soon is a status, not a separate page

Black Bear Diner is not built yet. status: planned lets its page be written now and shown as a card that does not link anywhere until you flip it to open.

Placeholder content

The Piroshky Piroshky description and hours are written to fill the layout. Real copy and real hours come from the business.
